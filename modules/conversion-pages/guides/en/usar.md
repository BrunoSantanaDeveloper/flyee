# Pages that sell

This app helps your AI build pages that sell the way good sales teams do, and check each one before it goes live. It works in Portuguese and follows Brazilian consumer law.

## What to ask

Tell your AI which page you want, what it sells and where it sits in your site or in the purchase flow. Examples:

- "Create the product's sales page, with demo booking and the trial period."
- "Build the plans page with monthly and yearly billing and the comparison table."
- "Make the sales page for my mentoring program, with the Black Friday offer in 12 installments and the bonuses."
- "Make our API page for developers, with the plans and the documentation."
- "Create the upsell page shown after checkout, offering the group mentoring at 50% off the first month."
- "Add an order bump to the course checkout with the launch checklist for R$ 19.90."
- "Review my plans page before I publish it."

## The page types

| Type | Where it goes |
|---|---|
| Sales page | the product's main address, or where campaigns and links point |
| Course sales page | the address that ads, the email list and the launch send buyers to |
| Plans and pricing | the plans page, or the plans section of the sales page |
| API page | the page for developers, tied to the documentation |
| Order bump | a box in the checkout, right above the pay button |
| Upsell | right after the payment is confirmed, before the thank-you page |
| Downsell | once, for whoever declined the upsell |
| Cross-sell | on the product page, in the cart or on the thank-you page |
| Thank-you page | the end of the order |

## What to expect

1. Your AI looks at what the project already has: the pages, the brand's look, the plans, the checkout and the payment provider.
2. It asks, in a single message, only what is missing, each question with a suggestion: the product, the price, the plans, the trial period, the guarantee, the button text.
3. It writes the page plan: the headline, the figure of each section, the visual direction, the sections in order, how the purchase works, what to measure and the lines the law requires. In the chat you see three lines and pick the headline from the suggested one and two others.
4. It builds the page in your project, mobile first, with figures that make sense without reading: a phone with the conversation, a calendar, the large window of the system.
5. It opens the finished page, looks at the page with no letters on it, at the photos and at the demo's frames, and checks every item of the type. You hear only what needs attention, and it fixes it.

## What the app never does

- Leave a paid box already checked.
- Charge without showing the amount first.
- Hide the decline option, or word it in a way that shames.
- Show a struck-through price or a countdown unless you confirm they are real.
- Show a discount or savings figure that does not add up.
- Open a promotion window over the page the moment someone arrives.
- Show in a figure what the product does not do yet.
- Remove from a finished page what you chose, unless you ask.
- State a number, a scarcity or a name without knowing where it came from.
