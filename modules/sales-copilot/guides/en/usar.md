# How to use it

Sales copilot follows your sales calls by your own playbook. It reviews a call you have already had, practises with you before a hard one and, during the call, shows on a page what to ask and what to answer. You leave each call knowing what was missing, with the sentence ready for the next one.

The app reads calls in any language, and its documents and suggested sentences come out in Portuguese for now.

## Ask like this

> "Review this sales call: transcripts/clinic-call.txt. I am the seller, Rafael."

> "Build the sales playbook for my business."

> "I want to practise a call with a client who finds it expensive and has to talk to a partner."

> "I am about to join a sales call. Open the live copilot."

> "Of the calls we have reviewed, what do the ones that closed have in common?"

## What happens

**Reviewing a call.** You bring the transcript: the file your meeting tool exports, or a text with one turn per line. If the names do not make it clear who is selling, the AI asks once who the seller is. Then it reads the call turn by turn and shows three lines: the call's signal, what weighed most against it and what was missing from the roadmap. The full document stays in the app's folder.

**Building the playbook.** The AI looks in your project for what you sell and asks, in a single message, what only you know: the price you say on a call, the next step you usually propose and what clients compare you with. Then it writes each move's sentences in your voice. If you do not know an answer, say so and it moves on.

**Practising.** The AI suggests a scenario and plays the client. You write what you would say, and after each round it shows the signal, the open objection and the next move. At the end you get the same document a real call gets.

**Live.** The AI asks whether the client has been told the conversation will be transcribed. With your yes, it opens a page in your browser. On the page you turn your microphone on, share the audio of the meeting's tab so the app can hear the client, and keep the page next to the meeting. After each turn it shows the stage, the open objection, what is still to be asked and the next move, with a sentence to say. At the end, click the button that ends the call and go back to the conversation with the AI.

The first time you use the live mode, the AI opens another page for you to paste the OpenRouter key, which reads each turn, and offers to do the same with the Gemini key, which transcribes the audio. The keys never go through the conversation.

## What you get

| Where | What |
|---|---|
| In the conversation | three lines about the call and what is yours to decide |
| The copiloto-de-vendas folder, inside calls | one folder per call, with the document, the turns and the reading |
| The copiloto-de-vendas folder | your business playbook |

A call's document carries the stages of the conversation, the roadmap item by item with the question that was missing, the objections and how each one ended, how much each side spoke and the turns that weighed most, with the move for each.

## Tips

- Tell the outcome of each call when you know it: it became a sale or it did not. With the outcomes of several, the app compares the signal of the ones that closed with the ones that did not.
- Build your playbook early. With your own sentences, the live suggestion sounds the way you speak.
- Use headphones on a live call. Without them, your microphone picks up the client's voice and the turns get mixed.
- If a turn came out under the wrong person, the page has a button that fixes the last one.
- In a meeting in person, one microphone serves both: tick that option on the page and use the S key to switch who is speaking.

## Limits

- The call's signal is a rule of thumb. It shows which way the conversation is going and does not predict whether the sale closes.
- Two readings of the same call can give slightly different numbers, because the notes on tone and on how much the client takes part depend on who reads. The objections and the roadmap items vary less.
- A single call is an indication. Comparing calls only says something with the outcomes of several of them.
- A recording with no transcript cannot be reviewed yet.
- On a live call, the app does not separate two voices on the same microphone by itself.
- The app sends no message, books no meeting and does not talk to the client. It reads the conversation and suggests.
- The document comes out in Portuguese.
