# Pages that sell, under the hood

## The repertoire

The app carries a playbook for each page type: sales page, course sales page, plans and pricing, API page, order bump, upsell, downsell, cross-sell and thank-you page. Each playbook covers:

- what the page is and when to use it;
- the rule that never breaks;
- the sections in order, the mechanics and the copy;
- the motion, mobile, measurement and the legal lines.

Real models come with it, analysed one by one. Each model records the source, the date it was seen, the page in order, the mechanics worth taking, the measured motion and what not to repeat. The app carries the analysis, never another company's full text. Today's models:

- Hostinger's post-purchase offer for its AI agent, with the second offer and the thank-you page;
- the sales page, the plans page and the API page of Juridiq, software for law firms;
- the sales page of JuriChat, the WhatsApp support product from the same group;
- the sales page of Subido AI, a course with a platform, mentoring and tools, in both versions of its A/B test of the top, seen on November 9, 2026;
- the product pages of Databricks, a data and AI platform, with demo graphics drawn from the product's own parts, its own icons and real logos, seen the same day.

The first four were seen on November 7, 2026.

## The stages

1. **The type:** your AI recognises the type from your request. A sales page that receives a paid ad is built here, and the continuity between the ad and the page belongs to the ad creatives app.
2. **The facts:** one file per page holds the product, who it is for, the price, the plans, the trial period, the regular price when one is struck through, the renewal, the guarantee, the deadline, the button texts and how to cancel.
3. **The plan:** the headline, the figure of each section, the visual direction, the sections in order, the mechanics, the measurement events, the legal lines, the origin of each number and a list to check by reading the code.
4. **The build:** in your project's code, with the components and the look it already has.
5. **The check:** the finished page opens in a windowless browser, on desktop and on mobile. Each item of the type comes back ok, attention or not checked.
6. **The look:** the check measures rules and cannot see whether the page makes sense. So your AI opens the page with no letters on it, the photos and the demo's sheets of frames, answers whether each figure still says what it is and whether the headline sounds like speech, and fixes the largest fault it sees first, in up to two rounds. On a page that already existed, it takes a photo before changing anything and compares afterwards.

## The headline

The headline promises what changes for whoever arrived, in a sentence someone would say. Your AI writes at least five, in different forms, and tests each one: out loud, swapping the product's name for a competitor's, looking for words only the maker uses, and checking whether the scene can be pictured. It brings the suggested one and two others, and the choice is yours. The exaggeration stays in the interest, never in the facts.

## The figures

People look before they read. Each section shows an object recognised in three seconds: a phone with the conversation, a calendar with the days marked, a chart, the large window of the system. A card with a sentence inside is not a figure.

The figure is drawn for someone who has never seen the product, with its colors, its type and its words. The real screen comes in when it makes sense at the page's size, and your project's component when it already is a recognisable object. Everything a figure shows exists in the product today, and whatever is invented for it carries the label "Exemplo fictício" (fictional example).

The guide holds the measures: up to 15 words per sentence inside a figure, 3 to 5 moments in 10 to 16 seconds in an animated scene, a design of its own for the phone and a cover that tells the story alone. The main features get one row each, with the large figure beside them. The smaller ones end in up to three short cards.

The look follows, in this order, your request, the project's design manual and the model you chose.

## The patterns AI repeats

The app carries a list of what shows up when an AI writes with nobody asking: a round number with no origin, invented scarcity, a founding year worn as a badge, three equal cards, a gradient heading, glow, blurred blobs, verbs that fit any product. Invented data leaves the page. The rest becomes a warning with a question, so the AI does not add it by reflex. What came from your request, the manual or the model stays, and on a page that already exists nothing leaves unless you ask.

What says whether the page sells is your project's own measurement.

## The check items

| Item | What it checks |
|---|---|
| button on the first screen | the main button shows on mobile without scrolling |
| main heading | the page has a heading marked for search engines and screen readers |
| one decision per page | on an offer, no other purchase action competes with it |
| visible, neutral decline | there is a decline link, worded without shaming |
| price, struck price and renewal | the registered amount shows; the struck price matches the real one; a subscription says how it renews |
| discount math | the discount, the savings in reais, the monthly average and the installments' total add up with the page's prices |
| cash price | the cash price or the total shows next to the installments, never the installment alone |
| value list | the total written is the sum of the items |
| plans | the prices on the first screen and one plan highlighted |
| trial and promotion | the trial says how many days it lasts; the promotion says when it ends |
| cancellation | the page says how to cancel and whether there is a lock-in |
| who sells | the company registration number, the address and a contact show on the page |
| promotion window | it has a way out and does not cover the page on arrival |
| numbers | no number shows as zero before its animation |
| guarantee near the button | the guarantee sits in the same part of the page as the button |
| charge stated | near the button, the page says that accepting uses the order's payment |
| sticky bar | on a long page, mobile shows a bar with the button |
| proof about the product | the testimonials are about the product on offer |
| countdown | only with a real, registered deadline |
| checked box | no box with a price comes checked |
| order bump near payment | the box sits right above the pay button |
| API page | the documentation on the first screen, a request example and the usage limits in numbers |
| video and motion | autoplay video is muted and has a cover image; with reduced motion asked, nothing keeps moving by itself |
| legal lines | privacy, terms and how to withdraw |
| images | alternative text on images; on a phone, images up to 500 KB and at the size they appear |
| numbers and urgency | each with its origin recorded, and no sample data left behind |
| headline that promises | the headline does not open with the category's name without speaking to whoever arrived |
| figures | each figure in the plan is marked on the page and holds no long sentence to read |
| cards | none asks for more than 32 words of reading or has a hole in it; testimonials, questions and plans are left out |
| demo | marked, with alternative text, no heading inside and the example label; filmed on desktop, on a phone and still with less motion |
| AI patterns | those visible on the finished page, except the ones the plan chose, always as a warning |
| calls to action | at most two texts for the same request |
| brand | the page's colors with a hue and its fonts are in the recorded brand |
| phone width | nothing passes the screen's width |
| legible text | prices and buttons at 12 px or more, and under 40% of the text below that |
| touch | targets of 24 by 24 px, or with room around them (WCAG 2.2) |
| contrast | 4.5 to 1, or 3 to 1 for large text (WCAG 2.2); text over an image you check in the photos |
| buttons | the text fits, neither cut nor touching one side |

Anything the capture cannot read comes back as not checked, never as a failure.

## The law

The app follows the Brazilian Consumer Protection Code and the e-commerce decree, with the articles checked on the version's date:

- clear price information;
- what the page promises becomes part of the contract;
- misleading advertising;
- products that were never asked for;
- the 7-day right of withdrawal;
- the seller identified, with the offer's conditions and restrictions;
- a summary before the contract;
- a withdrawal that undoes what was bought alongside.

It is a method to get the basics right, not legal advice.

## Pages behind a login

A page that only opens in the middle of an order can be saved in the browser as a single-file web page. Your AI checks that file the same way.

## Privacy

Everything stays in your project: the facts, the plan, the check and the captured screens. The check's photos and films stay out of your commits. A capture of a page saved while logged in may include order details. It stays in the page's folder and never leaves the project.
