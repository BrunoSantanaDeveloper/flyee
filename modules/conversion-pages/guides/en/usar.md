# Pages that sell

This app helps your AI build offer pages the way good sales teams do, and check each one before it goes live. It works in Portuguese and follows Brazilian consumer law.

## What to ask

Tell your AI which page you want, what it offers and where it sits in the purchase flow. Examples:

- "Create the upsell page shown after checkout, offering the group mentoring at 50% off the first month."
- "Add an order bump to the course checkout with the launch checklist for R$ 19.90."
- "Make the thank-you page for the purchase, with the first step to access the course."
- "Review my upsell page before I publish it."

## The page types

| Type | Where it goes |
|---|---|
| Order bump | a box in the checkout, right above the pay button |
| Upsell | right after the payment is confirmed, before the thank-you page |
| Downsell | once, for whoever declined the upsell |
| Cross-sell | on the product page, in the cart or on the thank-you page |
| Thank-you page | the end of the order |

## What to expect

1. Your AI looks at what the project already has: the pages, the brand's look, the checkout and the payment provider.
2. It asks, in a single message, only what is missing, each question with a suggestion: the product, the price, the guarantee, the button text.
3. It writes the page plan and sums it up in three lines. The plan covers the sections in order, how the purchase works, what to measure and the lines the law requires.
4. It builds the page in your project, mobile first.
5. It opens the finished page and checks every item of the type. You hear only what needs attention, and it fixes it.

## What the app never does

- Leave a paid box already checked.
- Charge without showing the amount first.
- Hide the decline option, or word it in a way that shames.
- Show a struck-through price or a countdown unless you confirm they are real.
