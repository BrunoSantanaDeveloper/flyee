# Pages that sell, under the hood

## The repertoire

The app carries a playbook for each page type: sales page, plans and pricing, API page, order bump, upsell, downsell, cross-sell and thank-you page. Each playbook covers:

- what the page is and when to use it;
- the rule that never breaks;
- the sections in order, the mechanics and the copy;
- the motion, mobile, measurement and the legal lines.

Real models come with it, analysed one by one. Each model records the source, the date it was seen, the page in order, the mechanics worth taking, the measured motion and what not to repeat. The app carries the analysis, never another company's full text. Today's models, all seen on November 7, 2026:

- Hostinger's post-purchase offer for its AI agent, with the second offer and the thank-you page;
- the sales page, the plans page and the API page of Juridiq, software for law firms;
- the sales page of JuriChat, the WhatsApp support product from the same group.

## The stages

1. **The type:** your AI recognises the type from your request. A sales page that receives a paid ad is built here, and the continuity between the ad and the page belongs to the ad creatives app.
2. **The facts:** one file per page holds the product, who it is for, the price, the plans, the trial period, the regular price when one is struck through, the renewal, the guarantee, the deadline, the button texts and how to cancel.
3. **The plan:** the sections in order, the mechanics, the measurement events, the legal lines and a list to check by reading the code.
4. **The build:** in your project's code, with the components and the look it already has.
5. **The check:** the finished page opens in a windowless browser, on desktop and on mobile. Each item of the type comes back ok, attention or not checked.

## The check items

| Item | What it checks |
|---|---|
| button on the first screen | the main button shows on mobile without scrolling |
| main heading | the page has a heading marked for search engines and screen readers |
| one decision per page | on an offer, no other purchase action competes with it |
| visible, neutral decline | there is a decline link, worded without shaming |
| price, struck price and renewal | the registered amount shows; the struck price matches the real one; a subscription says how it renews |
| discount math | the discount, the savings in reais and the monthly average add up with the page's prices |
| plans | the prices on the first screen and one plan highlighted |
| trial and promotion | the trial says how many days it lasts; the promotion says when it ends |
| cancellation | the page says how to cancel and whether there is a lock-in |
| who sells | the company registration number, the address and a contact show on the page |
| promotion window | it has a way out and does not cover the page on arrival |
| numbers | no number shows as zero before its animation |
| guarantee near the button | the guarantee sits in the same part of the page as the button |
| charge stated | near the button, the page says that accepting uses the order's payment |
| sticky bar | on a long page, mobile shows a bar with the button |
| proof about the product | the testimonials are about the product on offer |
| countdown | only with a real, registered deadline |
| checked box | no box with a price comes checked |
| order bump near payment | the box sits right above the pay button |
| API page | the documentation on the first screen, a request example and the usage limits in numbers |
| video and motion | autoplay video is muted and has a cover image; the reduced motion rule is there |
| legal lines | privacy, terms and how to withdraw |
| images | alternative text on images |

Anything the capture cannot read comes back as not checked, never as a failure.

## The law

The app follows the Brazilian Consumer Protection Code and the e-commerce decree, with the articles checked on the version's date:

- clear price information;
- what the page promises becomes part of the contract;
- misleading advertising;
- products that were never asked for;
- the 7-day right of withdrawal;
- the seller identified, with the offer's conditions and restrictions;
- a summary before the contract;
- a withdrawal that undoes what was bought alongside.

It is a method to get the basics right, not legal advice.

## Pages behind a login

A page that only opens in the middle of an order can be saved in the browser as a single-file web page. Your AI checks that file the same way.

## Privacy

Everything stays in your project: the facts, the plan, the check and the captured screens. A capture of a page saved while logged in may include order details. It stays in the page's folder and never leaves the project.
