# Pages that sell, under the hood

## The repertoire

The app carries a playbook for each page type: order bump, upsell, downsell, cross-sell and thank-you page. Each playbook covers:

- what the page is and when to use it;
- the rule that never breaks;
- the sections in order, the purchase mechanics and the copy;
- the motion, mobile, measurement and the legal lines.

Real models come with it, analysed one by one. Each model records the source, the date it was seen, the flow, who sees the offer, the page in order, the mechanics worth taking, the motion frame by frame and what not to repeat. The first one is Hostinger's post-purchase offer for its AI agent, seen on November 7, 2026. The app carries the analysis, never another company's full text.

## The stages

1. **The type:** your AI recognises the type from your request. The landing page of a paid ad belongs to the ad creatives app.
2. **The facts:** one file per page holds the product, the price, the regular price when one is struck through, the renewal, the guarantee, the deadline, the button text, the decline text and whether accepting charges in one click.
3. **The plan:** the sections in order, the mechanics, the measurement events, the legal lines and a list to check by reading the code.
4. **The build:** in your project's code, with the components and the look it already has.
5. **The check:** the finished page opens in a windowless browser, on desktop and on mobile. Each item of the type comes back ok, attention or not checked.

## The check items

| Item | What it checks |
|---|---|
| button on the first screen | the accept button shows on mobile without scrolling |
| one decision per page | no other purchase action competes with the offer |
| visible, neutral decline | there is a decline link, worded without shaming |
| price, struck price and renewal | the registered amount shows; the struck price matches the real one; a subscription says how it renews |
| guarantee near the button | the guarantee sits in the same part of the page as the button |
| charge stated | near the button, the page says that accepting uses the order's payment |
| sticky bar | on a long page, mobile shows a bar with the button |
| proof about the product | the testimonials are about the product on offer |
| countdown | only with a real, registered deadline |
| checked box | no box with a price comes checked |
| order bump near payment | the box sits right above the pay button |
| video and motion | autoplay video is muted and has a cover image; the reduced motion rule is there |
| legal lines | privacy, terms and how to withdraw |
| images | alternative text on images |

Anything the capture cannot read comes back as not checked, never as a failure.

## The law

The app follows the Brazilian Consumer Protection Code and the e-commerce decree, with the articles checked on the version's date:

- clear price information;
- misleading advertising;
- products that were never asked for;
- the 7-day right of withdrawal;
- a summary before the contract;
- a withdrawal that undoes what was bought alongside.

It is a method to get the basics right, not legal advice.

## Pages behind a login

A page that only opens in the middle of an order can be saved in the browser as a single-file web page. Your AI checks that file the same way.

## Privacy

Everything stays in your project: the facts, the plan, the check and the captured screens. A capture of a page saved while logged in may include order details. It stays in the page's folder and never leaves the project.
