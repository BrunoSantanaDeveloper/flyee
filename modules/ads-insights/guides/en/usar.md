# How to use

Before the first real is spent, your AI gets measurement ready: what counts as a result, what is on your page, the links of every ad under one rule, the test script and the ledger where each lead and purchase enters with its origin. Afterwards, you export the table from the Ads Manager and it reads: each ad's verdict, with the certainty the sample allows, the weekly read and what to do.

## Ask like this

- "Check whether measurement is ready before I turn the campaign on. This is the page: [link]."
- "Build the ad links the right way."
- "I exported the campaign table. What do we scale, what do we cut, what can't be read yet?"
- "Build the weekly read."
- "Is anything on fire today?"

## What happens

1. The AI agrees the measurement contract with you, in one message: what kind of business it is, which business number judges the ads, where the origin is recorded when someone signs up. What the plan already answered it does not ask.
2. It reads your page and says what is there: Meta Pixel, Google Analytics, Tag Manager, Google Ads tag, cookie notice. It also says what that does not prove: whether a tag fires only the platform's own test shows, and it writes that test script for your business.
3. It builds the ad links under a single rule and checks the ones you already have. A link without the five fields does not go live.
4. It opens the origin ledger. Each lead and purchase enters with date, origin and a pseudonym in place of the person; name, e-mail and phone are never written. A list exported from your tool enters whole, with the origin columns only.
5. After a week of campaign, you export the ad-level table from the Ads Manager and save it in the inbox folder. It links each line to the delivered piece and judges: scale, hold, iterate, cut or insufficient data, with the certainty and where the piece fails.
6. Every week it crosses the table with the origin ledger, says how much more the platforms claim than you sold, judges last week's moves and proposes this week's, each with why, certainty, check date and trip-wire. In the conversation come three lines and the decisions; the document stays in the project.
7. You make the moves in your account and say you did. Fourteen days later, the read says whether it worked.

## What you receive

| Where | What |
|---|---|
| ads-insights/medicao.json | the measurement contract, each item marked verified, informed or unknown |
| ads-insights/rastreamento.md | your business's tracking plan, with the test script |
| ads-insights/origens.csv | the origin ledger, with no personal data |
| ads-insights/leituras/(reading)/leitura.md | each ad's verdict, with the numbers and the reason |
| ads-insights/semanal/(date).md | the weekly read: findings, decisions, the story, what to do, what not to do, what was missing |
| ads-insights/pulso/(date).md | the day's pulse, only when something broke a threshold |

## Tips

- Always export the whole campaign period, at ad level, with impressions, link clicks, landing page views, 3-second plays and results. What is missing stays blank, never zero.
- Save the export in ads-insights/entrada before asking for the read; a list with personal data is only read from there.
- When the AI shows the two readings of an ad at the threshold, the choice is yours: it does not decide in silence.
- After changing an ad set, wait for the date the move becomes readable before changing it again.

## Limits

- Below the minimum sample the read is an indication, and the text says so. With a week of a small budget, the most common answer for the final event is "insufficient data".
- Meta's and Google's counts are claims; the truth is the origin ledger.
- The app does not touch your ad account, does not send e-mail or messages and asks for no password.
- The thresholds are labelled, dated rules of thumb; your account's history counts more once it exists.
- The app speaks Brazilian Portuguese: the documents are written in Portuguese, and amounts follow the Brazilian format (R$ 1.490,00). Exports in English are read as well.
